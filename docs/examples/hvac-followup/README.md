# Your follow-up coordinator

*Example takeaway for a fictional guest (Dana, Eastgate Heating & Air). This shows what a non-technical owner would receive with the free configuration file. It is a design. Nothing has been built or switched on.*

## What this is

A design for an AI employee that keeps your open quotes and unpaid invoices moving, so you stop spending about ten hours a week chasing people.

## What it would do

- **Quote follow-ups.** If a customer has not replied three days after a quote, it sends a short, friendly follow-up in your voice. If there is still no reply after seven days, it sends one more. Then it stops.
- **Invoice reminders.** Seven and fourteen days after an invoice is overdue, it sends a polite reminder. Then it stops.
- **Every evening,** it emails you a short list: what went out today, who replied, and the few things that need your decision.
- **Every Friday,** it sends a one-page summary so the rules can be tuned.

## What it will always bring to you instead of acting

- Anyone who mentions a discount or a payment plan
- Anyone who is upset or disputes a charge
- Anything that would be a third message
- Any invoice over the limit you set
- Customers you mark as "do not contact automatically"

## What it will never do

- Mention fees or legal action
- Quote or change a price
- Change anything in your records
- Take a payment
- Contact anyone outside business hours

## How you stay in control

- **Approvals.** Each evening you get a short email. Anything that needs your decision has a link: approve it, edit it, or discard it. Priya can do the same. Nothing waiting for approval is ever sent on its own.
- **Limits that cannot be skipped.** The two-message limit, business hours and the invoice limit are enforced by a small program, not left to the AI's judgment, so a mistake in judgment still cannot send a third message.
- **A pause switch.** One step stops everything. Ask us and we turn it off.
- **Your data.** By default, message text is deleted after 90 days and only the record of what was sent is kept. You decide.

## How much time it might save

We estimate **6 to 8 hours a week** of the ten you described. This is an estimate, not a promise. It assumes about 60 follow-ups a week and that about 70 percent of them are routine. We would check both numbers after the first month. Running costs (AI usage and hosting) are measured over a trial week and shared with you before anything is switched on.

## What it would need from you

- Read access to your field-service app and your accounting software
- Permission to read and send email, and your choice of whether messages go out from your own address or a dedicated one
- The invoice amount above which you want to approve reminders yourself
- Ten minutes to read and correct the tone guide before anything is sent

## What you are getting today

- This design, and the file next to it (`blueprints.json`) that spells out every choice and the reason for it.
- You can hand that file to Claude, or to us, as the plan for building this employee.
- It does not switch anything on. Building and running it is a separate step.

## Two other options we considered

- **Drafter.** It only prepares drafts and you send every one. It is the safest, but because you read email late in the evening, follow-ups would go out a day late.
- **Back-office lead.** It also answers replies, books callbacks and updates your records. It would save the most time, but it needs connections we do not yet support, and it speaks for you more than we recommend to start.
